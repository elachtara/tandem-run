# Tandem Run

**A women-only running and walking partner app, launching in Boston, Summer 2026.**

Built by women, for women. Because running is better as two.

## About

Tandem Run is a women-only running and walking buddy app. We match you with one perfect partner near you: same pace, same schedule, same vibe. We supply the routes, you just pick a time and show up.

Most running apps are built for groups or solo tracking. Tandem Run is built for two. It's for the runs you've been cutting short, the routes you've been avoiding, and the early mornings you've been skipping because going alone didn't feel worth it.

## How it works

1. **Build your profile.** Neighborhood, pace, time of day, and overall vibe. These details help us pick your perfect match.
2. **Verify your identity.** All members verify their identity through the Persona ID verification flow. We verify identities, but do not conduct background checks.
3. **Meet your match.** We match you with one other highly compatible woman in your area, and supply one of our hand-curated routes.
4. **Make it happen.** Propose a time to meet up in the app and she accepts. Simple as that, no texting required.

## Who it's for

Tandem Run is open to all who identify as women, including transgender women and nonbinary individuals who feel comfortable in a women-centered space. Everyone is welcome at any ability level, including guide running, wheelchair users, stroller-pushers, and anyone who moves differently.

## Safety

We've thought carefully about safety from day one. Every profile is verified through Persona, every match is 1-on-1, and we suggest meeting points in public, well-trafficked areas.

## Routes

The site includes a curated collection of running routes across Boston, Cambridge, Somerville, and the inner suburbs. Every meeting point is chosen with safety in mind. Browse them at [tandemrun.app/routes](https://tandemrun.app/routes/).

## This repo

Static marketing and waitlist site for the Tandem Run pre-launch, hosted on GitHub Pages at tandemrun.app. It includes:

- The homepage waitlist landing page, collecting name, email, pace, preferred run time, and Boston neighborhood.
- A curated routes browser at `/routes`, with individual route pages and Leaflet maps.
- Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages.

Form submissions are sent to a Google Sheet via Google Apps Script. Visitor analytics use the HubSpot tracking code, gated behind cookie consent.
